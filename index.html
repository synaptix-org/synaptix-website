Platform Capabilities

Built for Rigorous
Neuroscience Research

Every component is designed around the demands of peer-reviewed research — reproducible, validated, and extensible.

Real-Time Signal Streaming

Ingest and visualize up to 256-channel EEG streams at full bandwidth with automatic impedance monitoring and live artifact flagging.

Automated Preprocessing

PREP pipeline, ICA decomposition, ASR, and bandpass filtering — configurable per-paradigm with full audit trails and parameter logs.

Epoch & Event Segmentation

Define epochs by trigger codes, response markers, or custom logic. Epoch rejection and interpolation are logged and reproducible.

Spectral & Time-Frequency

FFT, Welch PSD, Morlet wavelets, and ERSP/ITC — with interactive topographic maps exportable in standard neuroimaging formats.

Source Localization

LORETA, sLORETA, and dipole fitting with MRI-registered head models, compatible with MNI-space atlases and Talairach coordinates.

Report Generation

LaTeX-ready figures, BrainVision and EDF/BDF exports, and machine-readable JSON summaries compatible with BIDS standards.

How It Works

From Raw Signal to
Analysis-Ready Data

A four-stage workflow designed to help labs move from raw EEG recordings to clean, documented datasets for downstream analysis.

1

Ingest

Import EEG recordings and organize metadata, channel information, and study details in a consistent format.

2

Clean

Apply lab-calibrated preprocessing steps such as filtering, artifact screening, bad-channel review, and optional ICA-based workflows.

3

Review

Inspect quality-control outputs, understand preprocessing decisions, and adjust parameters when needed.

4

Export

Export cleaned data, preprocessing logs, and QC reports for analysis in tools such as EEGLAB, MNE, or your lab's existing workflow.
